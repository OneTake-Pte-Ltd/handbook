A meeting recap, with these sections in this order:

1. **Meeting:** its name, date and attendees.
2. **Context:** two or three sentences on why the meeting took place.
3. **Decisions:** what was decided, as a list.
4. **Action items:** a table with the columns Action, Owner and Due date.
5. **Open questions:** what is still to be decided or checked.

If the draft contains a transcript, put the full transcript, cleaned of filler words and false starts but otherwise unchanged, in a closed collapsible section (`details` with `summary` "Transcript") at the end of the last step.
