A policy document, with these sections in this order:

1. **Purpose:** why this policy exists.
2. **Who it applies to:** the people, roles or teams concerned.
3. **The policy:** clear rules, written as short statements of what people must and must not do.
4. **Responsibilities:** who does what to apply the policy.
5. **Exceptions:** when an exception is possible and how to request one.
6. **If the policy isn't followed:** the consequences.
7. **Related documents:** other policies or procedures to read, when the draft names any.
