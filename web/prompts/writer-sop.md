A standard operating procedure (SOP), with these sections in this order:

1. **Purpose:** what this procedure achieves and why it matters.
2. **When to use it:** the trigger, the situation or event that starts it.
3. **Who does it:** the role responsible.
4. **Before you start:** the access, tools and information needed.
5. **Procedure:** numbered steps (`ol`), one action per step, in the order they are done. Put warnings that prevent mistakes in a `aside data-callout="warning"` next to the step they concern.
6. **How to check it worked:** what the result should look like.
7. **Common problems and fixes:** a short list, or a table with the problem and the fix.
